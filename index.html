About the writer

A short note on my background.

I'm a writer and communications professional working across reported journalism, policy analysis, and corporate storytelling. I studied English at the University of Guelph, where I graduated with high honours and learned, more than anything, to take other people's arguments seriously before reaching for my own.

My work has spanned operations and communications roles at Mobikasa and The Vault Co., where I learned to translate messy internal information into clear external language, which is a habit that still shapes how I write. I am most interested in stories where the technical and the human aren't actually separate questions.

I am available for commissions, contract work, and conversation.
